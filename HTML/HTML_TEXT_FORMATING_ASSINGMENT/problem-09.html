Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

1. Students must carry their valid college ID card while attending classes.

2. Students must attend classes regularly and follow the instructions given by faculty members.

Attendance

3. Students are required to maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each subject.

Students with attendance below the required percentage may not be permitted to appear for the final examination. Attendance will be calculated at the end of the semester.

Examination

4. Students must carry their college ID card and examination admit card during examinations.

5. Any form of cheating or use of unauthorized material during an examination is strictly prohibited.

Note: Students should reach the examination hall at least 15 minutes before the scheduled starting time.

Assignment Submission

6. All assignments must be submitted according to the instructions provided by the faculty.

Assignment submission deadline: 5:00 PM on the assigned due date.

Late submissions may receive reduced marks. Assignments submitted more than 3 days late may not be accepted.

Discipline

7. Students must maintain proper discipline and respectful behaviour inside the college campus.

Students must not damage college property or disturb other students during classes.

Note: Students are expected to maintain a positive and respectful learning environment.

Important Notice

All students are responsible for reading and following these rules.
