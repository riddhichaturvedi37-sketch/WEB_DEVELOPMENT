GLA University

About

GLA University is a leading institution located in Mathura, Uttar Pradesh. It is known for its commitment to academic excellence and holistic development. The university provides a vibrant campus life, modern infrastructure, and a strong focus on research and innovation.

Programs

The university offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, and Computer Applications. Specialized courses in Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, and Cyber Security prepare students for future technologies. Each program emphasizes practical learning and industry exposure.

Campus Life

The campus is equipped with advanced laboratories, libraries, and sports facilities. Students participate in cultural events, technical fests, and community service programs. The environment encourages creativity, teamwork, and leadership.
