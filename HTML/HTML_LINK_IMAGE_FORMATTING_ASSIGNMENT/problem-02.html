GLA University Launches AI Innovation Hub

Empowering Students with Cutting‑Edge Technology

GLA University has announced the opening of its new Artificial Intelligence Innovation Hub on August 27, 2026. The initiative aims to provide students with hands‑on experience in machine learning, robotics, and data science. Faculty members believe this step will revolutionize technical education in the region.

The hub features advanced computing labs, collaborative workspaces, and industry‑grade equipment. Students will engage in real‑world projects under expert mentorship, preparing them for global tech challenges. The university’s leadership emphasized that innovation and creativity are at the heart of this program.

The inauguration ceremony was attended by leading researchers and industry professionals. They highlighted the growing importance of AI in transforming businesses and society. GLA University plans to host annual hackathons and workshops to nurture young talent.
