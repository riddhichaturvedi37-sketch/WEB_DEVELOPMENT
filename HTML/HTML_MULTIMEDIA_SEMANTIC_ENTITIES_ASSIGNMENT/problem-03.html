Destination Overview

The Andaman & Nicobar Islands are a tropical paradise located in the Bay of Bengal, known for their pristine beaches, coral reefs, and lush rainforests. Visitors can enjoy snorkeling, scuba diving, and island hopping while experiencing local culture and cuisine.

Travel Tip: The best time to visit is between October and May for pleasant weather and calm seas.
