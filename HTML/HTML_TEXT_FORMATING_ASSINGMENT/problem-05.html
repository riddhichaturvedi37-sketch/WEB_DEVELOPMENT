Science Notes

Chemistry

Water is a chemical compound made up of two hydrogen atoms and one oxygen atom: H2O.

Carbon dioxide: CO2

Oxygen: O2

Sulfuric acid: H2SO4

Ammonia: NH3

Atoms are the basic units of matter and can combine to form molecules.

Physics

Speed is the distance travelled per unit of time.

Area = length × width = m2

Volume = length × width × height = m3

Acceleration = m/s2

The speed of light is approximately 3 × 108 m/s.

Gravity is the force that attracts objects towards each other.

Biology

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants prepare their food using sunlight.

Plants use carbon dioxide (CO2) and water (H2O) during photosynthesis.

DNA carries genetic information in living organisms.
