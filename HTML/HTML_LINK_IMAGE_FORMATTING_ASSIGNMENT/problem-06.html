TechFest 2026 - Innovate, Create, Inspire

Introduction

TechFest 2026 is GLA University’s annual technology festival celebrating innovation and creativity. Students from across India will showcase projects in AI, robotics, and sustainable tech. The event aims to inspire young minds to build solutions for a smarter future.

Highlights

The festival features coding competitions, robotics challenges, and startup exhibitions. Expert workshops and tech talks will provide hands‑on learning experiences. Special attraction: Drone Racing and AI Hackathon with exciting prizes!

Date & Venue

Date: October 10–12, 2026
Venue: GLA University Campus, Mathura, Uttar Pradesh
All events will be held in the Innovation Block and Auditorium.

Speakers

Renowned industry leaders and alumni will share insights on emerging technologies. Keynote sessions include talks by experts from Microsoft, Google, and ISRO. Interactive Q&A sessions will help students connect with professionals.
