GLA University Admission Handbook

Admission Overview

GLA University Greater Noida offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in Engineering, Management, and Computer Applications. The admission process is transparent and merit‑based, ensuring equal opportunity for all applicants. Application deadline: September 15, 2026.

Eligibility

Candidates must have passed Class 12 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics for B.Tech programs. For postgraduate courses, a relevant bachelor’s degree is required. Minimum aggregate marks of 50% are mandatory for all programs.

Application Process

  1. Visit the official website and register for admission.
  2. Fill out the online application form with accurate details.
  3. Upload required documents and pay the application fee.
  4. Download the confirmation receipt for future reference.
  5. Attend the counselling session as per the schedule.

Required Documents

  • Class 10 and 12 Mark Sheets
  • Transfer Certificate and Migration Certificate
  • Passport‑size Photographs
  • Government ID Proof (Aadhaar Card)
  • Entrance Exam Score Card
