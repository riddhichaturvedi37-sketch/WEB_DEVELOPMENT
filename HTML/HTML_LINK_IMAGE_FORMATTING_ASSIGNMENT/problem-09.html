About Us

Blue Tokai Coffee Roasters brings the art of specialty coffee to Greater Noida. Known for its freshly roasted beans and sustainable sourcing, the café offers a warm, modern space for students and professionals. Every cup is brewed with care, combining rich aroma and authentic Indian coffee culture.

Signature Items

Our signature drink is the Cold Brew Coffee — smooth, bold, and refreshing. Pair it with our Almond Croissant or Avocado Toast for a perfect breakfast. Special Offer: Enjoy 20% off on your first order this month!
