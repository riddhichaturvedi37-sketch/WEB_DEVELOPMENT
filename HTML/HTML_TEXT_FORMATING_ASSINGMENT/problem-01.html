About the College

GLA University is a well-known educational institution that provides quality education and helps students develop their academic and professional skills. The college offers a positive learning environment, experienced faculty members, and various opportunities for students to grow in their careers.

Courses Offered

The college offers various undergraduate and postgraduate courses. Some popular courses include Computer Science and Engineering, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Information Technology, Mechanical Engineering, Civil Engineering, and Management courses. These courses help students gain theoretical knowledge as well as practical skills.

Campus Life

Campus life is full of learning, activities, and new experiences. Students can participate in cultural events, technical fests, sports, workshops, seminars, and different student clubs.
The campus environment also helps students make new friends and develop communication and teamwork skills.
