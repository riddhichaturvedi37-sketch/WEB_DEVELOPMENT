About Institution

GLA University Greater Noida is an extension of GLA University Mathura, established to provide world‑class education in the NCR region. The campus offers modern infrastructure, advanced labs, and a vibrant learning environment. It focuses on emerging fields like Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science.

Principal’s Message

Welcome to GLA University Greater Noida! Our goal is to nurture talent and inspire innovation. We encourage students to think critically, act ethically, and lead confidently. Join us to shape your future in technology and research.

Academic Programs

The campus offers specialized programs in B.Tech (AI & ML), B.Tech (Data Science), and BCA (Cloud Computing). Each program integrates theory with hands‑on projects and industry exposure. Students gain practical skills through internships and collaborative research.

Campus Facilities

The Greater Noida campus features smart classrooms, innovation labs, and Wi‑Fi‑enabled study zones. Students enjoy a safe, eco‑friendly environment with modern hostels and cafeterias. GLA University ensures comfort and creativity in every corner.

Student Life

Life at GLA Greater Noida is dynamic and interactive. Students participate in tech fests, cultural events, and sports activities. Clubs like AI Society and Coding Circle help students explore new technologies. The campus encourages teamwork and leadership through every initiative.
