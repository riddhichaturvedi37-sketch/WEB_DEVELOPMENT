Practical Class Preparation Steps

  1. Review the lab manual and assigned tasks.
  2. Set up your coding environment (VS Code or Jupyter Notebook).
  3. Write and test your programs before submission.
  4. Document your observations and results clearly.
  5. Submit your lab report to the faculty on time.

Classroom Rules

  • Be punctual and attend all lectures regularly.
  • Maintain discipline and respect faculty members.
  • Keep mobile phones on silent mode during class.
  • Participate actively in discussions and projects.
  • Mandatory: Submit assignments before the deadline.

Academic Definitions

Lecture
Regular classroom session for theoretical concepts.
Lab
Practical session for hands‑on learning and experiments.
Tutorial
Interactive problem‑solving session with faculty guidance.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation based on assignments and tests.
Practical
Applied learning through projects and lab work.
