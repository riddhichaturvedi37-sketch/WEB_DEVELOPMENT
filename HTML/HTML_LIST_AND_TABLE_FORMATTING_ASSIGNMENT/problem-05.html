Fest Introduction

GLA University Greater Noida proudly presents its three‑day annual fest — a celebration of creativity, culture, and technology. Students from across India will participate in exciting competitions, workshops, and performances. Dates: October 10 – 12, 2026

Registration Process

  1. Visit the official fest portal at www.gla.ac.in.
  2. Fill out the online registration form with accurate details.
  3. Upload your college ID and payment receipt.
  4. Receive confirmation email with your participant ID.
  5. Report to the registration desk on the event day.

Fest Rules

  • All participants must carry college ID cards.
  • Maintain discipline and respect event coordinators.
  • Late entries will not be accepted after October 5, 2026.
  • Follow safety guidelines and instructions during events.
  • Decisions of judges will be final and binding.
