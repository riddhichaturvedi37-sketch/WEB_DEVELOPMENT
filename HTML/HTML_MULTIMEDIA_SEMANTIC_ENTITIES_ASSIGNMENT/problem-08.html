Financial Tips

  • Save at least 20% of your monthly income.
  • Maintain an emergency fund equal to 6 months’ expenses.
  • Compare loan offers before borrowing.
  • Pay credit card bills on time to avoid penalties.
  • Invest in diversified assets for long-term growth.

Account Opening Steps

  1. Choose a bank and account type.
  2. Fill out the application form.
  3. Submit KYC documents (ID & address proof).
  4. Deposit minimum balance.
  5. Receive account number and debit card.

Loan Terminology

Interest Rate
Percentage charged on borrowed amount.
EMI
Equal Monthly Installment = Principal + Interest.
Principal
Original amount borrowed.
Tenure
Duration of loan repayment.
